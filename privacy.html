Catafolio

Privacy Policy

Effective date: July 4, 2026

Catafolio helps users capture and organize product photos, then create catalogs, quotations, order forms, and PDF documents.

Data We Process

  • Product photos, product names, SKUs, prices, notes, customer details, PDF profiles, and drafts are used to create and manage your documents.
  • Camera and photo library permissions are used only when you choose to capture, select, or save images.
  • Subscription status is handled through Apple StoreKit. We do not collect your payment card number or Apple ID password inside the app.

Data Storage

  • Product images, drafts, PDFs, and settings are stored locally on your device by default.
  • When you delete products, drafts, or documents, the app attempts to remove the related local files.
  • The app uses local preferences for necessary state such as language, usage counts, and the last selected PDF profile.

Data Sharing

  • This app currently does not include a third-party advertising SDK.
  • This app currently does not upload your product photos, customer details, or PDF documents to a developer server.
  • When you share a PDF through the system share sheet, the selected app or system service handles that data.

Your Choices

  • You can disable camera or photo library access in iOS Settings.
  • You can delete products, drafts, and generated documents in the app.
  • You can manage or cancel subscriptions through your Apple ID settings.

We may update this policy as the app changes. Material changes will be reflected in app updates or in this published policy.
